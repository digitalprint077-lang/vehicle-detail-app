Route permits and fitness certificates for the Sindh desk.

Staff prepare the official vehicle record, save it, and print the A4 sheet. The printed document carries a public page with the registration, chassis, engine, and status.

What this desk handles

Two official records, plus the public check page.

Route permit

Owner name, CNIC or NTN, vehicle type, registration, and the route. Print it as the A4 summary that stays with the vehicle.

Fitness certificate

Registration, chassis, engine, issue date, expiry date, and the certificate status. Save it and print the same official sheet.

Public check page

The printed permit links to a page that shows the registration, chassis, engine, dates, and whether the record is valid.

For staff

From sign-in to the printed sheet.

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Open the desk with the admin username and password. A refresh signs you out.

  2. 2

    Fill the form

    Choose Route Permit or Fitness Certificate and enter the vehicle details.

  3. 3

    Save the record

    Saved permits and fitness records stay in the desk and can be backed up.

  4. 4

    Print A4

    Print the official sheet. Its check page is what the public opens.
