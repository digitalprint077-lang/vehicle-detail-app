Services

Everything the transport desk prints.

Two official records, prepared in one place, and a public page that confirms them.

Route permit

Owner name, CNIC or NTN, vehicle type, registration, and route. Printed as the A4 summary carried on the vehicle.

  • Custom vehicle types
  • R.P. number and validity

Fitness certificate

Registration, chassis, engine, issue and expiry dates, and the certificate status, on the same official sheet.

  • Valid, expired, or pending
  • Expiry tracked on the dashboard

Public check page

The QR code on a printed permit opens a page with the registration, chassis, engine, dates, and status.

  • No sign-in needed
  • Works on any phone

How it works

From sign-in to the printed sheet.

  1. 01

    Sign in

    Staff open the desk with the admin username and password. Refreshing the page signs out.

  2. 02

    Fill the form

    Choose Route Permit or Fitness Certificate and enter the vehicle details.

  3. 03

    Save the record

    Records are kept in the desk with a cloud copy, and can be downloaded as a backup.

  4. 04

    Print A4

    Open the print view and print the official sheet with its QR code.

Verification

Check a permit with your phone camera.

Scan the QR code printed on the permit. The check page shows the vehicle registration, chassis and engine numbers, issue and expiry dates, and whether the record is valid.
