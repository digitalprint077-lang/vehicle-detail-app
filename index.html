Services

Official vehicle records for Sindh.

Two official records, and a public page that confirms them.

Route permit

Owner name, CNIC or NTN, vehicle type, registration, and route. Printed as the A4 summary carried on the vehicle.

  • R.P. number and validity
  • Carried on the vehicle

Fitness certificate

Registration, chassis, engine, issue and expiry dates, and the certificate status, on the same official sheet.

  • Valid, expired, or pending
  • Issue and expiry dates shown

Public check page

The QR code on a printed permit opens a page with the registration, chassis, engine, dates, and status.

  • No sign-in needed
  • Works on any phone

How it works

From the issued permit to a verified record.

  1. 01

    Permit issued

    The Provincial Transport Authority issues the route permit or fitness certificate for the vehicle.

  2. 02

    Printed on A4

    The official sheet is printed with the vehicle details and a QR code.

  3. 03

    Scan the QR

    Point a phone camera at the QR code on the printed permit.

  4. 04

    See the status

    The check page shows the vehicle numbers, dates, and whether the record is valid.
